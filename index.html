Straight answers

The questions everybody asks

Why would you build it before I pay you?

Because explaining a website to somebody who does not want one wastes both our time, and building you one takes an afternoon. If you like it, you pay. If you do not, we lost an afternoon and you lost nothing. It is the same reason you would rather walk a job than talk about it on the phone.

I already have a Facebook page. Is that not enough?

It is better than nothing and you should keep it. But a Facebook page does not show up when somebody searches your trade plus your city, and it makes a property manager or a commercial account think twice. If you only ever want residential referral work, Facebook may honestly be fine, and we will tell you that rather than sell you something.

Can I edit it myself later?

Two separate answers, and we would rather be straight with you on both. Are you stuck with us? No. You own the files and the domain, it is standard code on standard hosting, and any developer can pick it up tomorrow. Can you click into it and change the text yourself? Not out of the box. Your site is hand built rather than assembled in a page builder, which is why it loads instantly and costs what it does, and hand built code has no login screen. We can add a simple edit-in-the-browser panel if you want one, which takes us a few hours and gets quoted separately. Almost nobody asks. Texting us the change is faster than logging into anything, and on the care plan it is already paid for.

What do you need from me?

Your license number, your service area, your phone number, and whatever job photos are already on your phone. If you have a logo, send it. If you do not, we will make you one. Total time on your end is about twenty minutes.

Do you run ads or do SEO?

We set the site up so search engines read it properly and we get your Google Business Profile in order, which is most of what actually matters for a local trade. We do not run ad campaigns and we will not promise you rankings, because anyone who does is guessing.

Is there sales tax on this?

Yes. Texas treats website design and hosting as a taxable data processing service, where eighty percent of the charge is taxable and twenty percent is exempt. It shows as its own line on your invoice so you can see exactly what it is.

What if I need it in a hurry?

Tell us. We have turned a site around in 48 hours for somebody with a bid due. There is no rush fee.
